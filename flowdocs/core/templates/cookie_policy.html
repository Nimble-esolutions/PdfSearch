1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are commonly used to make websites work securely and to remember choices you make.

2. How This Platform Uses Cookies

The Sahakar AI platform uses essential security cookies and, only after an explicit choice, limited first-party product-analytics cookies. Optional analytics is never required to search public documents or use public information pages.

We do not use advertising or targeting cookies, third-party tracking cookies, social-media cookies, session replay, behavioural profiling, or analytics that records search questions, answers, document names, or source URLs.

4. Optional Product Analytics

When enabled by an authorized administrator for the exact public host you are visiting, the Platform uses the self-hosted Umami service at analytics.ai-sahakar.net only after you select Accept optional analytics in the normal cookie notice. It sends a small, manually authored allowlist of content-free events such as interface presentation, language, result outcome, source-opening outcome, and coarse latency range. It never sends a search question, answer, document name, source URL, account identifier, page title, raw page URL, query string, referrer, session replay, or performance trace.

The raw browser token stays in the HTTPS-only, host-only cookie above and is never available to JavaScript or sent to Umami. The application sends only a versioned one-way pseudonymous alias. As with any direct browser connection, the self-hosted analytics service can receive connection metadata such as IP address and user-agent information. It must use its own approved retention and access policy.

Global Privacy Control always prevents optional analytics and removes the optional identity cookie. Do Not Track prevents collection until you explicitly accept optional analytics; that explicit cookie choice then takes precedence. If the analytics service is unavailable, public search continues normally.

5. Cookie Choices and Applicable Law

Cookie use is governed by applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000, to the extent applicable:

  • Essential cookies support the technical operation and security of the Platform.
  • Preference cookies record a choice you make in the interface.
  • Optional analytics cookies are set only after you accept them in the normal cookie notice. You can choose essential cookies only, or clear the site's cookies later, without losing public search access.

6. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, delete, block, or receive an alert before cookies are set.

Guides for managing cookies in common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Where optional analytics is available, choose Essential only in the normal cookie notice to decline it. After accepting, clear this site's cookies in your browser to withdraw the stored choice and identity; the notice will appear again on a later visit. This does not by itself delete events that the self-hosted analytics service has already received; retention and deletion requests follow the approved analytics operating policy.

7. Effect of Disabling Cookies

  • Admin users: You will not be able to log in or access the admin dashboard if required session or CSRF cookies are disabled.
  • Public users: Public search remains functional. Language and optional analytics choices may not be remembered between visits.
