1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide security features, and remember user preferences.

2. How This Platform Uses Cookies

The Sahakar AI platform uses only essential cookies — cookies that are strictly necessary for the Platform to function and to provide security. We do not use any of the following:

  • Advertising or targeting cookies
  • Third-party tracking cookies
  • Analytics or performance cookies
  • Social media cookies
  • Cookies for behavioural profiling or user tracking

4. Legal Basis for Cookies

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDA) and the Information Technology Act, 2000:

  • Essential cookies (session and CSRF tokens) are necessary for the technical operation and security of the Platform. They are set on the basis of our legitimate interest in maintaining a secure and functional service.
  • Preference cookies (language) are set only after you make a language selection. By selecting a language and continuing to use the Platform, you consent to the storage of this preference.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View the cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete individual cookies or all cookies.
  • Block cookies from being set (entirely or from specific websites).
  • Configure the browser to alert you when a website attempts to set a cookie.

Guides for managing cookies in common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

6. Effect of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies entirely, the following functionality may be affected:

  • Admin users: You will not be able to log in or access the admin dashboard, as session and CSRF cookies are required for authentication and security.
  • Public users: The public search interface should remain functional. However, your language preference will not be remembered between visits, and you will see the cookie consent notice on each visit.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Policy periodically.
