Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

1. Introduction

Sahakar AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDA) and applicable Indian laws.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Username, email, role, and department when you register an account.
  • Search Queries: Questions and keywords you submit to the AI search engine.
  • Uploaded Documents: PDF files uploaded by administrators for indexing.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, access timestamps for security and rate limiting.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your data for the following purposes:

  • To provide AI-powered document search for Maharashtra Cooperative Society laws.
  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access control.
  • To index and make searchable official government documents.
  • To prevent abuse through rate limiting and security monitoring.

4. Third-Party Services

We use OpenAI's API for AI-powered search responses. Search queries are transmitted to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI's servers may be located outside India. By using this service, you consent to this transfer.

5. Data Retention

User accounts are retained until deletion. Search queries are not permanently stored. Uploaded documents are retained as long as they remain indexed. Backup generations in our artifact vault follow a configurable retention policy.

6. Your Rights

Under the DPDA 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • File a grievance with our Grievance Officer
