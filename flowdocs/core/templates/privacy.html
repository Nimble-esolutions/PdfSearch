1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Office of the Commissioner for Cooperation and Registrar, Cooperative Societies, Maharashtra State, Pune ("Registrar, Cooperative Societies", "we", "our", or "us"), as the Data Fiduciary, collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data when you access or use the Sahakar AI platform at ai-sahakar.net (the "Platform").

This Policy is published in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDA), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (SPDI Rules), to the extent applicable.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this Policy, please discontinue use of the Platform.

2. Who We Are (Data Fiduciary)

For the purposes of the DPDA 2023, the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data is:

Registrar, Cooperative Societies, Maharashtra State
Pune, Maharashtra, India
Website: sahakarayukta.maharashtra.gov.in

Technology implementation and infrastructure management are provided by Nimble eSolutions, acting as a Data Processor under the instructions of the Data Fiduciary.

3. What Personal Data We Collect

The Platform serves two distinct categories of users. The personal data we collect differs accordingly:

3.1 Public Users (Unregistered)

When you use the public search interface without registration, we may collect:

Data CategoryWhat We CollectLegal Basis (DPDA)
Search Queries The text of questions you submit to the AI search engine. These are processed transiently and are not permanently stored. Consent (implied by use); Legitimate Use — public service delivery
Technical Data IP address (hashed for rate limiting), browser type, device type, access timestamps. Legitimate Use — security and abuse prevention under Section 7(g) DPDA
Language Preference Your choice of interface language (English or Marathi), stored via a cookie. Consent (via cookie notice)

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identifiers from public users. The Platform is designed for anonymous, registration-free access to government document search.

3.2 Authorized Officials (Registered Admin Users)

When you are registered as an authorized official with an admin account, we collect:

Data CategoryWhat We CollectLegal Basis (DPDA)
Account Information Username, email address, role (admin/superadmin), and department name. Consent (at registration); Legitimate Use — employment/government function under Section 7(i) DPDA
Login Credentials Password (stored as a salted cryptographic hash only; never in plaintext). Necessary for authentication and account security
Activity Data Document uploads, folder management actions, user management actions, and audit logs of administrative activities. Legitimate Use — government function and audit compliance
Session Data Authentication session tokens, IP address, login/logout timestamps. Legitimate Use — security of the Platform

4. Purposes for Which We Process Personal Data

We process personal data only for the following specified purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide AI-powered search of Maharashtra Cooperative Society acts, rules, circulars, and related government documents.
  • Authentication and Access Control: To authenticate admin users, enforce role-based permissions, and maintain the security of the admin dashboard.
  • Document Management: To enable authorized officials to upload, categorize, index, and manage government documents for public access.
  • Security and Abuse Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful activity through rate limiting and security monitoring.
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze anonymized, aggregate usage patterns for the purpose of improving the Platform. No individual user is identifiable from such analysis.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful government requests.

We do not use personal data for any purpose other than those stated above without providing additional notice and, where required by law, obtaining your consent.

5. Notice and Consent

In accordance with Section 5 of the DPDA 2023, this Privacy Policy serves as the notice provided to Data Principals (users) at or before the time of collection of personal data. It specifies:

  • The personal data to be collected (Section 3 above).
  • The purposes for which it is processed (Section 4 above).
  • The manner in which Data Principals may exercise their rights (Section 10 below).
  • The procedure for filing grievances (Section 11 below).

For admin users, consent is obtained at the time of account creation by the registering administrator. For public users, by using the Platform's search function, you provide your consent to the processing of your search queries and technical data as described in this Policy.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by discontinuing use of the Platform. For admin users, withdrawal of consent may result in termination of account access.

6. Disclosure and Transfer of Personal Data

6.1 Third-Party Service Providers (Data Processors)

We engage the following categories of Data Processors who may access personal data solely for the purposes of providing services to us:

  • AI Service Provider (OpenAI): Search queries submitted through the public interface and admin dashboard are transmitted to OpenAI's API for AI-powered response generation. OpenAI processes these queries under its own data processing terms. OpenAI's servers may be located outside India. This cross-border transfer is made with your consent, which you provide by submitting search queries. OpenAI's processing is governed by its Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.
  • Infrastructure Provider (Nimble eSolutions): Hosting, server management, and technical maintenance services. Nimble eSolutions processes data under a contractual data processing agreement and does not use personal data for its own purposes.

6.2 No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or otherwise disclose personal data to third parties for monetary or other valuable consideration. We do not share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or any entity not essential to the operation of the Platform.

6.3 Legal Disclosures

We may disclose personal data if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulation, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to: (a) comply with legal process; (b) protect our rights, property, or safety; (c) investigate and prevent fraud or security issues; or (d) respond to an emergency.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Platform uses only essential cookies necessary for its operation:

  • Session Cookie: Used to maintain authenticated sessions for admin users. This cookie is HttpOnly and transmitted over secure channels. It does not track public users.
  • CSRF Token Cookie: Used as a security measure to prevent cross-site request forgery attacks. This cookie does not contain personal data.
  • Language Preference Cookie: Stores your preferred interface language (English or Marathi).

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or any cookies for behavioural tracking or profiling. For full details, see our Cookie Policy.

9. Children's Data

In compliance with Section 9 of the DPDA 2023, the Platform is not directed at children (individuals below the age of 18 years). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The Platform's content pertains to cooperative society laws and government regulations and is intended for use by adults. If we become aware that a child has provided personal data through the Platform, we will take steps to delete such data promptly.

10. Your Rights under the DPDA 2023

As a Data Principal under the DPDA 2023, you have the following rights:

10.1 Right to Access Information (Section 11)

You have the right to request: (a) confirmation of whether we are processing your personal data; (b) a summary of the personal data being processed; (c) the processing activities undertaken; and (d) the identities of all Data Fiduciaries and Data Processors with whom your data has been shared.

10.2 Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12)

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data, and erasure of personal data, subject to any legal obligation requiring us to retain such data. For admin users, corrections to account information may be made by an authorized administrator.

10.3 Right of Grievance Redressal (Section 13)

You have the right to file a grievance with our Grievance Officer (see Section 11) regarding any matter related to the processing of your personal data.

10.4 Right to Nominate (Section 14)

You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights under the DPDA in the event of your death or incapacity.

10.5 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

10.6 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer at the details provided in Section 11. We will respond to your request within the timeframes prescribed under applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

12. Data Security

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures, in compliance with Section 8(5) of the DPDA 2023 and the SPDI Rules, to protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Cryptographic hashing of passwords (never stored in plaintext).
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.
  • Secure session management with HttpOnly and Secure cookie flags (in production).
  • Regular security updates and patching of software dependencies.
  • Network isolation of backend services from public internet exposure.

While we strive to protect personal data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Personal Data Breach

In the event of a personal data breach, we will take all reasonable steps to contain and mitigate the breach. In accordance with Section 16 of the DPDA 2023, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and, where required, affected Data Principals, of any breach that is likely to result in harm to the Data Principal.

14. Cross-Border Transfer of Data

By using the Platform, you consent to the transfer of your search queries to OpenAI's servers for the purpose of generating AI responses. We do not transfer any personal data of admin users outside India, except as described above for search queries.
